## Decision rule for the interviews (set before the first call, never change after)

Only money or a signed letter of intent counts as a commitment signal; verbal enthusiasm does not.

- **Persevere:** at least 10 of 20 describe an agent incident or near-miss unprompted, and at least 3 agree to a design-partner trial or LOI.
- **Pivot:** the pain is real but the policy and trace layer is not what they would adopt.
- **Stop:** fewer than 6 of 20 report any such problem.
